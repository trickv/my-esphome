AS3935 Lightning Sensor Placement Notes
=======================================

Problem: Sitting on a laptop with a 4" USB cable, the sensor is completely
overwhelmed by EMI noise — even with all thresholds maxed out.

Mounting Distance from Electronics
----------------------------------
- 1 meter (3 ft) minimum from electronics — huge improvement over being on a desk
- 1-2 meters is the sweet spot — moderate sensitivity, detects storms ~40km out
- Diminishing returns beyond 3 meters — noise is dominated by household wiring
  and appliances at that point, not the host device

What to Keep Distance From
--------------------------
- Switching power supplies (laptop chargers, phone chargers) — #1 indoor noise source
- WiFi routers
- Monitors/TVs
- The D1 Mini's own WiFi radio (use a 1-2m USB extension cable)

Placement Tips
--------------
- Near a window or exterior wall improves sensitivity to actual lightning
- Use a longer USB cable (1-2m) rather than the 4" one
- Indoor mode is set in config (indoor: true) which applies appropriate gain

Tuned Config (ground floor window, 1m from electronics, 2026-04-03)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  noise_level: 3       (range 1-7, floor at 2 — triggers disturbers)
  watchdog_threshold: 8 (range 1-10, floor at 7 — triggers disturbers)
  spike_rejection: 1   (range 1-11, clean all the way to minimum)

The watchdog_threshold is the dominant filter at this location.
spike_rejection had no effect — clean even at minimum.
noise_level floor is at 2 (one disturber per minute).

When moving to the attic (further from electronics), try reducing
watchdog_threshold first — it may be possible to bring it down to 5-6
which would improve detection range.
